## Key scientific findings

1. **Temporal (manual reps): filled in, didn't change.** Binning the S5 matrix
   by PDB deposition year, occupied-cell breadth saturates ~mid-2000s while hit
   depth keeps climbing; new-cells-per-1000-hits crashes from ~1000 (1980s) to
   ~0 by 2000. (`s5_timeseries.html`, figs.)

2. **Rarefaction: AFDB diverse per structure, but bounded.** AFDB accumulates
   matrix cells *faster per hitting structure* than PDB and overtakes it by
   ~10k structures; PDB is redundant (72k structures → 2,469 cells, plateaued).
   AFDB's lower *total* is shallower sampling, not less diversity.
   (`s5_rarefaction.png`.)

3. **Sequence-dark ≠ fold-dark (the spine).**
   - Unassigned AFDB dark matter **under-hits** S5 (~16% of DB → ~4% of hits);
     its 786 occupied cells are 96% PDB-known; only ~16 novel-vs-all-known,
     mostly singletons.
   - **TED novel folds** (7,427 curated, defined by *no CATH match*) → searched
     with ProSMoS: **89% on PDB-occupied cells; exactly 1 robust novel cell**
     (sk118/EEHHE). Even the most-curated fold-novelty set is geometrically
     known. Non-circular (S5 is classification-independent).
   - **3,380 / 6,336 cells** stay unoccupied across PDB + manual + AFDB(4.9M) +
     TED-novel — the hardened negspace. (Observation, not proof of
     unfoldability — see caveats.)

4. **Geometric determinism (method introspection).** Within-cell 5-SSE motif
   spread (fitted PCA axes, within-skeleton control, 16 skeletons / 426 cells):
   a **1-strand penalty** (loosest) then monotonic tightening to EEEEE
   (~-1.9 Å/strand). β-sheet H-bond registration is what makes a cell
   geometrically determinate; all-α / single-strand cells are grab-bags. So the
   descriptor's fidelity is **composition-graded**. (`s5_geom_determinism.png`.)

5. **ProSMoS is blind to transmembrane.** GPCR (T 5001.1.1) and MFS (T 5050.1.1)
   — maximally different TM folds — both land in the same generic all-helix
   `HHHHH` cells; indistinguishable at the S5 level. TM abundance differences
   are real (MFS ~4× AFDB-enriched; GPCRs *PDB*-enriched by pharma) but live at
   the T-group level, invisible at the cell level.

6. **The one robust AFDB-enriched signal is ARM repeats (109.4.1).** Confirmed
   via ECOD ("ARM repeat"). The well-populated AFDB-shifted cells are all
   `HHHHH`, dominated by 109.4.1 (~57k members, 2.7× AFDB). But it's **16×
   window-tiling** inflation: 3,521 ARM domains → 56,189 domain-cell hits
   spanning 801 cells (one domain hits up to 123 cells). Abundant, PDB-shy,
   messy-to-classify — not novelty.

7. **Annotation funnel cross-ref — capstone negative (resolved).** Found the
   `~/work/afdb_200M` GENUINE novelty funnel (dark clusters → dead-rep/prophage/
   InterPro/name/pLDDT filters). Direct ProSMoS S5 search of the 4,193 built v3
   reps (`~/work/prosmos_2026/s5_v3`, 96,191 hits, 2,063 cells) *appears* to
   break the pattern: **150 never-seen cells, 40 with ≥2 distinct proteins**
   (top HEEHE/sk117 = 6), and E-rich (26/40 have ≥2 strands — the geometrically
   *determinate* regime, not the all-α grab-bag). BUT this is an **input-
   granularity artifact**: v3 was searched as *whole multi-domain models* while
   the reference sets (pdb_exp, afdb) were *domain-chopped*. Novel-cell hits are
   enriched for large residue-spans (median 234 vs 153 res; 44% vs 24% span >250
   res) — 5-SSE motifs assembled across domain boundaries, which a domain-chopped
   search structurally cannot produce. **Controlling for compact (single-domain-
   scale) motifs collapses the signal monotonically**: ≥2-protein never-seen
   cells = 0 (≤120 res), 3 (≤150), 5 (≤180), 7 (≤200). At the generous 150-res
   cutoff the 3 survivors reduce to **2 geometries** (an HHHEH trio in adjacent
   skeletons sk17/sk24 = skeleton ambiguity, + an HEEEE pair), each ~123–146 res
   and backed by only 2–3 metagenomic proteins. So the most-curated annotation-
   funnel novelty set, searched at domain scale comparable to the reference,
   yields **essentially no robust PDB-negative S5 geometry** — the capstone
   negative. (Lesson for the paper: whole-model vs domain-chopped input must be
   matched, or cross-domain SSE assembly masquerades as fold novelty.)

   **SEALED (domain-chopped re-search).** To retire the "the compact cutoff was
   cherry-picked" objection, re-searched the 4,201 candidates chopped to their
   *own committed `_D<n>` boundary* (`afdb_200m.ecod_domain_range`; 4,196 built),
   rebuilt with the identical PALSSE+generateMatrix pipeline — a within-v3
   controlled A/B where the only variable is chopping. Result collapses exactly
   as predicted:
   |                        | whole-model | domain-chopped |
   |------------------------|------------:|---------------:|
   | occupied cells         |       2,063 |            982 |
   | total hits             |      96,191 |         16,981 |
   | never-seen cells       |         150 |             25 |
   | never-seen w/ ≥2 prot. |      **40** |          **0** |
   The 40 robust never-seen cells → **0**; the 25 residual never-seen cells are
   all singletons (noise floor, none reproduced across ≥2 domains). No post-hoc
   cutoff — the boundaries are the pipeline's own decomposition. The annotation
   funnel's most-curated novelty candidates, searched at their committed domain
   grain, produce **zero robust PDB-negative S5 geometry**. Build/sweep at
   `~/work/prosmos_2026/{v3_dom,s5_v3_dom}`.

   **SEALED (domain-chopped re-search, controlled A/B).** To remove any
   suspicion that the compact-motif cutoff was cherry-picked, re-searched the
   candidates chopped to their *own committed `_D<n>` boundaries*
   (`afdb_200m.ecod_domain_range`), same PALSSE+generateMatrix pipeline — only
   chopping changes. Result:
   | metric | whole-model v3 | domain-chopped v3 |
   |---|---|---|
   | occupied cells | 2,063 | 982 |
   | total hits | 96,191 | 16,981 |
   | never-seen cells | 150 | 25 (all singletons) |
   | **robust never-seen (≥2 proteins)** | **40** | **0** |
   Tracing the 40 robust whole-model novel cells through chopping: **32 vanish
   entirely** (motif spanned a domain boundary), **8 survive as singletons**,
   **0 stay robust**. Top cell sk117/HEEHE (6 "independent" whole-model proteins)
   → 0 chopped. Proven: the entire novel signal was cross-domain SSE assembly in
   whole-model input, not novel topology. Build/sweep at `~/work/prosmos_2026/
   v3_dom/` + `s5_v3_dom/`; diff `scratchpad/diff_v3dom.py`. This is the
   unimpeachable version — no judgment cutoff, the pipeline's own domain grain.

## Theory / framing (for a fold-space + domain-repertoire paper)

- **The negative is the result**: a *saturation measurement* using an
  assignment-free instrument (S5 = pure SSE geometry, enumerable → a
  denominator). Non-circular novelty test where CATH/ECOD-based ones beg the
  question.
- **ECOD "completed" — carefully bounded to 3 regimes**: (1) common core is
  discovery-complete; (2) the frontier moved from *discovery* to *membership*
  (AF = remote-homology-at-scale, mapping divergent members onto known bins);
  (3) the tail is curation-hard, not discovery-rich (repeats/symmetry/ARM).
- **Domain-repertoire frame**: sequence space (exploding) maps many-to-one onto
  a bounded, nearly-filled fold codomain that stopped growing orders of
  magnitude of sequence ago; AF is the lens that lets you see the collapse.
- **CASP rebuttal resolved**: CASP-novel = "no template at prediction time"
  (temporal), not topology-novel; AF's capacity is proven (CASP FM + de-novo
  design), so AFDB scarcity reflects the *input* (real proteome saturated), not
  model incapacity.
- **Honest limits (put in the paper)**: S5 is a *local, offset, fixed-cardinality*
  descriptor (Lesk critique) — claims are about local SSE-motif space; negspace
  is ambiguous (unfoldable vs unevolved vs never-frame-selected); tiling/offset
  inflate (ARM is the cautionary example) → controlled via compositional +
  within-skeleton normalization.
- **Compare metric must be compositional** (share of hits n/Σn), not
  per-structure rate (n/N): the rate metric is a near-uniform aggregate offset
  (pdb_exp vs afdb: mean +6.18, 1815/1815 same sign); compositional centers it
  (~50/50) and reveals cell-specific over/under-representation.
