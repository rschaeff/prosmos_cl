## The through-line: why AFDB "hits" S5 far less than PDB

Per-structure hit rate: pdb_exp **16–19%** vs afdb **0.55%** among ≥5-SSE
structures (~34×). This session proved the gap is **not incompleteness and not
missing folds** — it decomposes cleanly, and at the fold level PDB≈AFDB.

### Phase 0 — completeness audit → (c) "we didn't finish the search" is DEAD
- afdb DB has **4,921,931** real entries (`grep -c .ssd`); build essentially
  lossless (pdb_exp 99.2%, afdb ~100%).
- Sweeps complete: both DBs cover exactly 6,336 queries, 0 rc≠0, sane runtimes.
- `searchmatrix` **streams** the DB (searchControl.h:277), no entry cap → cannot
  truncate the 4.9M scan.
- Positive control: a specific afdb hit traced to a correct match.

### Phases 1–3 — the decomposition (redundancy is the driver)
- Input profiles: afdb median **5 SSE / 67-res span** vs pdb_exp **12 / 139**.
- **Size standardization**: applying pdb_exp size-binned rates to afdb's size
  dist → 9.82% expected vs 0.55% actual ⇒ size is only **~1.7×**, NOT dominant.
- **Redundancy (dominant)**: dereplicate pdb_exp by ECOD T-group → per-structure
  18.8% but only **1,360 distinct hitting T-groups** (52.9 entries/T-group).
  afdb: 0.55% but **1,086 T-groups** (14.1/T-group). **At the fold level they
  agree — AFDB recovers 80% (1,086/1,360) of PDB's S5-hitting T-groups.**
- 34× ≈ redundancy **3.8×** × size **1.7×** × denominator composition.

cells/T-group; curves track within ~15%. **AFDB T-groups are not cell-poorer.**

## Bottom line (the question that mattered): does AFDB reach topologies PDB can't?
**No — not robustly, at the S5 level.** AFDB is a ~80% *subset* of PDB's S5
cells sampled far denser; every novelty probe lands on known geometry; 3,380 /
6,336 cells stay empty for AFDB too. The only AFDB *surplus* is 27 all-**HHHHH**
cells — and that's a confound stack: the same α-solenoid/repeat families (ARM
109.4.1, Ankyrin 109.3.1, HEAT; X-group 109) + TM-helical (GPCR 5001, MFS 5050)
**tile overlapping 5-helix windows across many skeletons at once**, so one family
inflates every all-α cell (composition × S5-blind-to-helical-identity × tiling).
Two bounding caveats now on the "where it lands" slide:
1. **S5 is a local, fixed-cardinality descriptor** — "no new S5 cells" = no new
   *local 5-SSE motif* geometry, not "no novel global folds."
2. **AlphaFold is PDB-trained** — AFDB agreeing with PDB on reachability is partly
   a training prior; can't cleanly separate "proteome saturated" from "AF renders
   PDB-like geometry."

## ⚠️ RETRACTED this session: the "rep-truncation" finding

A deep dive claimed the searched afdb reps are systematically truncated fragments
(~half the length of their members; T-group ratios 0.26–0.55, worst for globular
folds; committed as 41c9323). **Quantifying the proposed fix exposed it as a
Simpson's-paradox weighting artifact** and it was **retracted (d6408c4)**:
- The wrong comparison: **cluster-weighted reps** (dominated by many small-domain
  clusters) vs **member-weighted members** (dominated by few huge long-domain
  clusters) — different populations.
- Proper **paired within-cluster** test: rep / member-median length = **0.99**
  (n=2,649). Clusters are length-homogeneous; reps faithfully represent them.
- The "fix" (rep → longest member) recovers only **1.09–1.20×** by cluster size,
  so the A/B search was **not run**.
- **Correct attribution (unchanged conclusion)**: afdb searched domains are short
  because of **dereplication** (cluster-weighted, small-domain-heavy cluster
  space), NOT rep truncation. Size 1.7× stands as a dereplication property;
  redundancy 3.8× is the driver. **Nothing to flag to grey** — reps are fine.
- Method note (recorded): rep-vs-member comparisons MUST be paired within-cluster.
- Illustrative figures kept but labeled atypical: `s5_rep_truncation.png`,
  `rep_truncation/renders/{cluster_violin,rep_vs_member}.png`.
- Grey schema learned: `partial_domains.parquet` (partial-domain flag),
  `dpam_prob_max`, `assign_status`=confidence(not completeness); reps are grey's
  mmseqs `358M→20M` representatives (we searched them, didn't pick them).
